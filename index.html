What UNetMine is doing

UNetMine estimates what the selected physical miner would produce today, subtracts modeled electricity and pool costs, and shows the resulting NET MINE amount as a purchase target you control.

Method: expected miner share = miner hashrate ÷ network hashrate; gross ZEC = share × modeled network miner rewards/day. Then pool fee and modeled electricity cost are deducted. Demo inputs are editable and not live feeds.
